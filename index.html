缘分 — Yuánfèn

An interactive audio-visual meditation on destined connection. Two glowing spheres of particles orbit each other in space. Holding your pointer acts as gravity, pulling them together. Double-tapping pushes them apart. As they draw close, the music shifts from minor to major. When they merge, they permanently exchange particles — carrying pieces of each other forward. Over time, the particles slowly erode — a meditation on impermanence.
