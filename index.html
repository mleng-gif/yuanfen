A NOTE ON THE WORK

To meet is to change.

Yuánfèn is an interactive meditation on affinity, agency, and the traces we leave in one another.

Draw the two presences closer. Give them space. Their movement belongs partly to you, and partly to themselves. When they meet, each carries a little of the other forward.

There is no correct ending.
